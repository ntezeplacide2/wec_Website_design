Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about WeCall services.

How does WeCall ensure Call quality and reliability?

WeCall uses enterprise-grade infrastructure with multiple redundancies, quality of service (QoS) protocols, and global points of presence to ensure crystal-clear voice quality and 99.99% uptime.

Can I keep my existing phone numbers?

Yes, we offer number porting services that allow you to transfer your existing business numbers to WeCall with minimal downtime during the transition.

What kind of support do you offer?

We provide 24/7 customer support through multiple channels including phone, email, and live chat. Our dedicated support team is trained to resolve issues quickly and efficiently.

Is WeCall secure and compliant?

Yes, WeCall is built with enterprise-grade security including end-to-end encryption, secure data centers, and compliance with major regulations like GDPR and HIPAA where applicable.

How easy is it to scale with WeCall?

Extremely easy. Our cloud-based solution allows you to add or remove users, features, and capacity with just a few clicks, with no additional hardware required.

Can WeCall integrate with my existing CRM?

Yes, WeCall offers seamless integration with popular CRM platforms like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho, as well as custom API options for proprietary systems.
