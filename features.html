Powerful VoIP Features

Discover how WeCall's advanced features can transform your business communications and drive growth.

IVR System

Automated Call routing for efficient customer service

Our Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system allows Callers to interact with your phone system through voice commands or keypad inputs, directing them to the right department or information without human intervention.

  • 24/7 automated customer service
  • Reduce Call handling time by up to 40%
  • Customizable menu options and prompts
  • Seamless integration with your CRM

Call Distribution

Intelligent routing for optimal customer experience

Our smart Call distribution system routes incoming Calls based on predefined rules, agent skills, availability, and customer value to ensure every Caller reaches the right person quickly.

  • Route Calls based on agent skills and availability
  • Prioritize high-value customers
  • Geographic routing based on Caller location
  • Time-based routing for after-hours Calls

Queue Management

Efficient handling of high Call volumes

Manage Call queues effectively with customizable hold messages, position announcements, and estimated wait times to keep customers informed and reduce abandonment rates.

  • Customizable hold messages and music
  • Position announcements in queue
  • Callback option to avoid waiting
  • Real-time queue monitoring for supervisors

CRM Integration

Seamless connection with your business tools

Integrate WeCall with your existing CRM to automatiCally log Calls, access customer information during conversations, and sync data across platforms for a unified customer view.

  • Automatic Call logging in CRM
  • Screen pop with customer information
  • Click-to-Call from CRM records
  • Support for Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho and more
