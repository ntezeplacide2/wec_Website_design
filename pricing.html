Pricing FAQs

Common questions about our pricing and plans.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, the new rate will be prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle. When downgrading, the new rate will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a contract or long-term commitment?

No, all our plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel at any time without penalty.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and for enterprise customers we can also issue invoices for payment.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes, we offer a 10% discount when you choose annual billing instead of monthly billing.

Is there a setup fee?

No, setup and installation are completely free for all plans. Our team will help you get set up and ensure everything is working properly.
